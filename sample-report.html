Prioritized findings

MediumSeveral service images are missing useful alt text

Example affected page: /services/example-service

Images that communicate meaningful content should have text alternatives. Missing alt text can make the page harder to understand for visitors using screen readers and can reduce the usefulness of image context.

Recommended review: Identify which images convey information and add concise alt text that describes their purpose. Decorative images can remain intentionally empty.

MediumA service page has no clear main heading

Example affected page: /services/example-repair

The page appears to begin with supporting copy but lacks a clear page-level heading. A strong heading helps visitors and assistive technology understand the page topic quickly.

Recommended review: Add one descriptive main heading that accurately names the service offered on that page.

LowTwo page titles are overly similar

Example affected pages: /about and /services

Page titles should help people distinguish pages in browser tabs and search results. Repeated or vague titles make that harder.

Recommended review: Give each important page a concise title that reflects its specific content and the business name where useful.

Suggested next actions

  1. Review the medium-priority structural and accessibility findings first.
  2. Confirm which image issues are meaningful versus intentionally decorative.
  3. Clean up page titles after the higher-impact items are resolved.
