Common questions

Do you need access to my website account?

No. The standard $50 audit is based on publicly available pages and does not require login credentials.

Does the audit automatically change anything?

No. It is read-only. Any website changes are a separate, explicitly authorized job.

What kinds of sites can you review?

The service is aimed at normal small-business websites with publicly reachable pages. If a site depends heavily on private dashboards, logins, or complex application workflows, I will scope that separately before taking on the work.

Can you fix the issues after the audit?

For small, clearly scoped HTML/CSS, content-structure, and accessibility fixes, yes. The exact work and price are agreed before any changes are made.
